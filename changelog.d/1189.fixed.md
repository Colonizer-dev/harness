**A question that closes without an answer is closed everywhere.** A question whose tool call got its result without an answer (an exec-policy ask that timed out or was refused) no longer stays "open" for autopilot while the Nest shows nothing. It closes with a `question_closed` event (`reason`: `tool_resolved`, `timeout` or `turn_end`), a restart or resume repairs such ghosts from the event log, and autopilot only waits on a question the colony's record shows as `waiting_for_answer`, so a finished colony verifies and publishes instead of sitting until it is parked.
