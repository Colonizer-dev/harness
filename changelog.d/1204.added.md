**The Model providers page is a quiet list, with credits left and when they reset.** Each provider is one row (logo, name, status dot, a gauge of the plan left, "resets in 3 h 12 min") that opens to a credits chart over the last 7 days with reset markers and a projected run-out, requests, failures and time per day as sparklines, the models ("3 enabled · +5 available"), which model settings use it and the connection details. The Mothership now keeps 30 days of per-day usage, plan-balance readings (read on Check and every half hour) and plan events, served by `GET /api/providers/{id}/usage?days=`; a plan balance reader can name a reset time with a new reset pointer. A provider with no balance reader says "balance unknown". ([#1204])
