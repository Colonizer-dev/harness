**Hide a GitHub org from Colonizer.** Settings → Workspaces → "Show or hide orgs" has a "Show in Colonizer" toggle per org, search, a "Hidden (n)" section and "Hide all without colonies"; the workspace switcher has "Manage orgs…". A hidden org leaves the switcher, the repository pickers, the backlog counts and the merge steward, and the `*` ("all repositories") choice of every loop and setting skips it, resolved when it is used. Nothing is deleted and running colonies keep running. ([#1213])
