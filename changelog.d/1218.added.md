**Spotlight: one bar to ask, search and act from anywhere.** ⌘K or `/`, the "Ask or search…" pill in the top bar, and the phone's floating button (or a pull down) open one frosted panel over the current page. One ranked list blends Go to (colonies, repositories, workspaces, Settings pages and fields, loops, chats), Do ("colonize #212", stop, resume, move to front, switch model, apply the update) and open Issues, ranked by what you seem to mean; an "Ask Colonizer" row is always last and streams its answer inline, then Expands into a full thread on the Chat page. Recents show when the box is empty and Up recalls the last question. The Colonize pane is now reached from Do (or its button). The Colonize issue list is friendlier: grouped by repository, a plain one-line summary and a state pill with a live dot per row, status, label and priority filters, Colonize, Move to front, Skip and GitHub on each row, and bulk actions.
