- **A first UHP surface: the mothership now answers the Unified Harness Protocol's read routes under `/uhp/v1`.** Discovery (`GET /uhp/v1/uhp`, no token needed), the installed harnesses, the model catalogue and the session listing — with version negotiation (`UHP-Version: 2026-09-12`), the UHP error envelope on every `/uhp` answer, and the same scoped-token limits as the `/api` routes. Streaming, cancellation and files are not served yet and are reported false in discovery on purpose; how the surface measures against the UHP conformance suite is in [docs/conformance.md](docs/conformance.md).
