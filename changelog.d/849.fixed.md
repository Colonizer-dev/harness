**The observability exporter stops re-413-ing a backend that has shrunk its request limit, and the health snapshot says how many records each signal dropped.** After a `413`, the exporter now remembers the smaller request size for that signal and builds its later batches within it, instead of rebuilding every batch at full size and being refused again on each tick (the refused batch itself is still bisected down to the offending record as before). The per-signal entries of `status.json` also gained `records_dropped`, `gaps` and `backlog_bytes`, so a backend's `dropped` counts, the holes in what could be read and each signal's share of the unread bytes are visible at a glance.
